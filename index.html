01 / THE AGENT

Planning support, with a human lead.

The Agent organizes the event brief and proposes a practical session flow. The organizer confirms local details, reviews access needs, and approves the plan.

Purpose-led

Connect activities to a clear outcome participants can understand and take away.

Time-aware

Fit the agenda to the available duration, including transitions and preparation.

People-first

Consider the audience, format, access needs, and participant instructions.

+
02 / WORKFLOW

From a rough idea to a reviewable plan.

The process makes unknowns visible and sends incomplete briefs back for clarification before a full plan is drafted.

STEP 01

Receive the brief

Collect the goal, audience, format, timing, and available resources.

STEP 02

Clarify gaps

Ask about essential unknowns; label assumptions clearly.

STEP 03

Set the outcome

Define what participants should learn, make, or experience.

STEP 04

Compose the flow

Sequence activities, durations, transitions, and facilitator cues.

STEP 05

Check feasibility

Compare with time, staffing, materials, and access needs.

STEP 06

Prepare notes

Draft a setup checklist and participant information.

STEP 07

Invite review

Share the draft and flag organizer decisions.

STEP 08

Revise & hand over

Apply feedback; call it final only after approval.

+
03 / DELIVERABLES

A practical handoff package.

✓
Timed run-of-show

Activity, purpose, duration, transitions, and facilitator prompt.

✓
Setup checklist

Room arrangement and materials drawn from the supplied brief.

✓
Participant note

What to expect, what to bring, and details still to confirm.

✓
Open questions

Assumptions, unknowns, and decisions for the organizer.

05 / TRUST & SCOPE

Clear about the limits.

Within the Agent’s role

Organize supplied details, draft and revise workshop materials, identify missing information, and explain trade-offs.

Organizer remains responsible

Confirm venue, budget, participant needs, suppliers, and final agenda. The Agent does not book resources or send invitations.

Does this website run the Agent?

No. It is a static showcase with a small, prewritten demo; it does not call an AI model.

Does the sample connect to Lark or Hermes?

No. This website does not connect to Lark or Hermes and does not send prompts or messages.

Who approves the event plan?

The organizer. The Agent prepares a draft; people confirm local details and make final decisions.
